<p><b>Are you using your phone, or is your phone using you?</b></p>
<p><i>Sukun Minimalistic Launcher</i> is a minimal Android launcher focused on calm defaults, low visual noise, and faster everyday navigation.</p>
<p><br><b>FEATURES:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Minimal home screen:</b> Keep your launcher clean and distraction-light.</li>
<li><b>Custom app setup:</b> Rename apps, hide unused apps, and control home-screen shortcuts.</li>
<li><b>Useful gestures:</b> Swipe actions, double tap actions, and quick launcher interactions.</li>
<li><b>Focus tools:</b> Focus mode, status-bar control, and screen-time-aware usage.</li>
<li><b>Optional extras:</b> Weather, prayer times, azan reminders, daily notes, and wallpaper support.</li>
<li><b>Privacy:</b> Your data stays on your device.</li>
</ul>
<p>Sukun Minimalistic Launcher aims to stay simple while still offering practical features for daily use.</p>
<p><b>Accessibility Service:</b> Accessibility is only used for gesture-related features such as screen lock and recent-app actions. It is optional and does not collect or share personal data.</p>
