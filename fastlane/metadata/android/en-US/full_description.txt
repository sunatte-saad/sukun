Sukun — A Minimal Launcher for Focus & Prayer

Your phone should work for you, not against you.

Sukun is a minimalist Android launcher designed to help you stay focused, present, and consistent with your prayers and daily goals. It replaces your distracting home screen with one that shows only what matters.

A HOME SCREEN DESIGNED FOR FOCUS

Keep your home screen clean with your chosen apps, time, weather, and essential information.

• Pin 1–8 favorite apps
• Customize text size and alignment
• Swipe left or right to open your chosen apps
• Swipe up for the full app drawer
• Swipe down for notifications or search
• Double tap to lock or start Focus mode
• Clock styles, date, battery, and status bar options

Simple by design.

PRAYER TIMES & SALAH TRACKING

Prayer times are calculated from your location, with a live countdown to the next salah.

• Mark prayers as completed or missed
• View prayer analytics (today / month / year)
• Receive Azan notifications
• Choose recordings from Makkah or Marylebone Mosque
• Upload your own Azan audio
• Optional hourly chime for gentle reminders

Sukun helps you build awareness without guilt or pressure.

MINDFUL MORNING

Start your day before the scrolling begins.

Mindful Morning prevents access to selected distracting apps, such as social media, for a set period after you wake up. Instead of immediately reaching for social media, you can use your first moments for prayer, planning, reflection, exercise, or simply starting your day calmly.

FOCUS MODE

Create intentional time away from distractions.

Activate Focus Mode with a double tap or from settings, choose a duration, and Sukun blocks app launches and can silence notifications using Android's accessibility service.

Use it for prayer, work, study, reading, or family time.

APP COOLDOWNS

Some apps are designed to keep your attention.

Set daily launch or usage limits for individual apps. Once a limit is reached, Sukun enforces a cooldown before the app can be opened again.

Hide unused apps, rename apps, and use Private Space support for a cleaner drawer.

REMINDERS & TODO LIST

Stay organized without another app. Create reminders and simple todo items, see pending tasks on your home screen, and receive reminder notifications.

SCREEN TIME

See your daily screen time on your home screen, plus today and 7-day analytics — an honest view of how you spend your time.

WEATHER

See current weather and temperature on your home screen. Use your location, choose a city, or open Google Weather. Updates run efficiently in the background.

PERSONALIZATION

Choose light mode, dark mode, or Ambient Light Theme, which adapts using your phone's light sensor. Optional daily wallpapers keep your home screen fresh. Multiple languages with RTL support.

PRIVATE & LIGHTWEIGHT

No ads. No account required to use the launcher.

Core launcher data stays on your device. Optional Google Sign-In is available only for backing up and restoring your settings across devices.

Accessibility is used only for lock, recents, and Focus Mode — it does not collect or share personal data.
